## Qualification guardrails

Research only = relevant research input without enough evidence for a higher qualification.

Qualified workflow evidence = a real current workflow or concrete failure is described from relevant operational experience.

Design-partner candidate = an organization-level discussion exists about testing a defined workflow.

Concrete workflow commitment = a defined non-production test, pilot, or integration experiment is agreed.

Never upgrade status based only on positive sentiment, a form submission, a demo visit, a social-media reply, a generic statement that the product is useful, or a job title without workflow evidence.

## Public claims rule

Whenever a finding is used publicly, retain enough internal traceability to answer who provided it, what they actually reported or demonstrated, and which part of the claim it supports. Do not expose confidential respondent details.
